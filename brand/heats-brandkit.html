Stamp

Every verified finish

Every finish counts, at any pace. Each stamp is one heat in its color, dated, and never repeats.

Medal

Milestones and moments

Your first heat, a PR, five heats, every borough. Some medals reward showing up, not winning.

Share

Built to be posted

New stamps and medals come with a story card. Every post shows the next heat and its date.

Community

Run with your city.

Heats is a running club without the club: no dues, no tryouts, no pace groups to keep up with. You show up at a park on Saturday and there are 40 people at the line.

Every pace

No cutoff time, ever. Walk it if you want. Your stamp looks the same.

Last one in, loudest cheer

Everyone waits at the line until the last runner finishes. That runner earns the Closer medal.

Coffee after

Every heat ends at a local spot nearby. The race takes 25 minutes and the hang lasts an hour.

Example: 212 runners have stamped a passport this fall, across 7 parks in 4 boroughs.

Brand basics

Volt is the brand. The parks bring the other colors.

The page is always white and type is always ink. Volt belongs to Heats itself. Each event color belongs to one park and appears only on that heat's pages, stamp and share cards.
